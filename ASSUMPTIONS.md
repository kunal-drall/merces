# ASSUMPTIONS.md

Every sensible default chosen where the brief was ambiguous, with its id, the place it is enforced, and what would
change if Kunal or counsel decides otherwise. Money and charter semantics that could change who gets paid or how
much are flagged **ASK** and were not decided silently: the default is implemented but marked for confirmation.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A-ID-1 | Identifiers | EVM addresses are normalised to lowercase in the domain model; Solana addresses are case-sensitive base58. `seriesId = "<chain>:<address>"`. | `@merces/domain` ids.ts | default |
| A-TERMS-1 | Terms | Stablecoins have 6 decimals (USDC, USDG). Live adapters read `decimals` from the mint/contract and refuse a series whose mint disagrees. | simulator + adapters | default |
| A-TERMS-2 | Lockup | "18 months" = 18 × 30 days = 540 days, stored per series in seconds. | `DEFAULTS.lockupSeconds` | default |

| A-C6-1 | Period "paid" | A period's `paid` is the gross the vault accepted; the issuer's cap refund is recorded separately (PaymentRefunded) and not counted as paid. | simulator `payIn`, indexer | default |

| A-M5-2 | Attestor role | `POST /admin/attestors` grants the `attestor` role at proposal time; the attestor can only act on chains where the multisig's `registerAttestor` has landed (checked against `listAttestors()`). Deactivation does not remove the role. | E13, E10 | **ASK** |
| A-M5-3 | Hardship decisions | Only the chosen attestor's own wallet can decide (the chain requires that signature); an admin may read the ciphertext metadata but never decide. | E10 | default |

| A-M5-5 | Annual true-up | Estimated = Σ(payment due − accrual + split received) over the year's accepted periods; overpayment is credited to the next period, underpayment is due within 60 days without accrual; the module records amounts and deadlines, the correction itself is a report for the next period (the chain has no "corrected report" command). | E10 | default (A-C8-3) |
| A-M5-6 | Reconciliation | `GET /admin/accounting/reconcile` persists and audits a run (the brief specifies GET). Fee sweeps out of the collector show as a negative delta until a sweep ledger exists. | E11 | default |
| A-REV-1 | Attestation scope | The verifier attestation signs (seriesId, periodId, incomeCommitment, tier) only; which provider the income came from never enters the attestation or the `submitReport` command (B7). | A-REV-2 | De-allowlisted winners | A bidder removed from the allowlist between reveal and settlement is excluded at settlement (not a revert): their deposit becomes refundable and the auction clears over the remaining revealed bids. | simulator `settleAuction` | default |
| A-REV-3 | Accrual at payment | Late accrual (C8) is computed at pay-in time from the actual lateness, not frozen at report acceptance. An approved pause suspends accrual: the due and grace deadlines shift by the paused seconds that overlap the time since the period ended (an open pause counts up to now, C8.5), so accrual stops during the pause, also on a period already past grace, and resumes when the pause ends. Period reads show the live due amount. | simulator `payIn`, `accrualNow`, `pausedSecondsSince`; `@merces/math` `deadlinesFor` | default |
| A-REV-4 | Cap arithmetic | The accepted amount on the capping payment is floored to what the remaining cap covers; the vault never accepts a rounding-dust overpayment. | `@merces/math` `applyGrossPayment` | default |
| A-REV-5 | Transfer-window escrow | Units committed to a sell order stay in the seller's position and are escrowed (not removed); only filled units move at clearing, unfilled escrow is released, and the seller cannot double-commit escrowed units. | simulator `commitWindowOrder`, `clearTransferWindow` | default |
| A-REV-6 | Extinguishment of an open raise | Extinguishing a series whose auction has not settled fails the raise (all deposits refundable) rather than being refused; a Draft series is cancelled. | simulator `extinguish` | default |
| A-REV-7 | Live eligibility | Backer-tier access and terms disclosure check the `eligibility` table on every request, so a revoke takes effect immediately; the `eligible` flag in the session token is informational only. | A-REV-8 | Idempotency replays | Stored idempotency responses never carry decrypted vault material (application notes are nulled before storage); a replay returns the redacted copy. | `@merces/core` `withIdempotency` | default |
| A-REV-9 | Secrets at startup | Development defaults for the session secret, vault key and local KMS seed are accepted only while every chain is the simulator; with any live chain a missing, short (< 32 chars) or default value stops the process. | `@merces/ports` `resolveSecret`, API and keeper entrypoints | default |
| A-REV-10 | Holder tier | `holder` = a position with units, claims, redemptions or burns in the series. A bid alone does not count; bidders see their own bids and refunds through `/me`. | `@merces/api` access tiers | default |
| A-REV-11 | Idempotency semantics | Keys are scoped per account (and per series for reports).
