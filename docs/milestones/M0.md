# M0 — Foundations

**Built.** pnpm/TypeScript monorepo; `@merces/math` (C3 payment, C5 fees, C6 cap and reward-per-share, C7
redemption and tail, C8 deadlines and accrual, C9 averaging, auction clearing; 26 tests); `@merces/domain`
(42 canonical events + 36 commands as zod schemas, `ChainPort`, state shapes, canonical hashing for
commitments and attestations, generated `schema/domain-v0.schema.json`); `@merces/simulator` (every command
of Part C in one engine with snapshot-and-rollback transactions, time travel, `solana` and `monad`
personalities, Postgres persistent mode, scenario harness; 19 scenario tests covering the full cycle on both
personalities, failed auctions, caps, lateness, pauses, redemption, tail, amendments, extinguishment, FX
fallback, transfer windows, tier-1 reconciliation and C11 invariants); `SolanaChain` and `EvmChain`
skeletons with mainnet guards and health checks; `@merces/chains` mode selection (mock/live/auto with
per-chain health gating); synthetic fixtures (12-issuer cohort, Stripe-shaped data); `stage1:sync` with all
six steps implemented against a synthetic Stage 1 drop and "Stage 1 not present" when absent; CI and a
scheduled `stage1-sync` workflow; Docker Compose.

**Assumed.** See ASSUMPTIONS.md (A-AUC-1, A-C8-1, A-C8-2, A-RED-1, A-RED-2, A-C11-6 are flagged ASK).

**Open.** Live adapter reads/writes wait for Stage 1 clients; Groth16 verification is a mock until the
circuit exists; Pyth FX feed ids beyond EUR/GBP/AUD must be resolved at runtime; no Chainlink FX on Monad.
